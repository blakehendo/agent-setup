# The weekly X thread

Friday, in the 14:20–17:00 SHIP block. Twelve weeks, twelve threads. Built from the week's five notes so it isn't extra work.

## Steps

1. **Read all of this week's notes** in `notes/` (Monday through Thursday, plus whatever exists for Friday).

2. **Find the one surprising thing.** A thread has exactly one claim. Scan the notes for the moment something didn't work the way it was supposed to, or a number that was bigger or smaller than expected. That's the thread. Everything else is supporting material or gets cut.

3. **Draft 6–10 posts:**
   - **Post 1 is the claim, with a number or a concrete failure.** Not a preamble, not "I've been exploring…". The most interesting sentence goes first.
   - Posts 2–8: what you built, what broke, what you measured. One idea per post.
   - Last post: what you're doing next week, and a link to the repo.

4. **Attach evidence.** A screenshot of the terminal, a chart, a table, the actual repo. A thread with no artifact reads like an opinion.

5. **Show it in chat for editing.** Do not post it. Blake posts.

## Rules

- **The quality bar is deliberately low.** Shipping weekly matters more than any individual thread being good. If it's taking more than 45 minutes, publish what exists.
- No thread about a thing that isn't built. If the week's build slipped, the thread is about why it slipped — that's a real post.
- Audience is crypto-native and fintech-adjacent, a few thousand followers from the 0x era. Assume they know what an RFQ is and what a stablecoin is. Don't explain basics.
- Tag the relevant project or team when there's a real reason to (a bug found, a spec followed, a result about their protocol). Not otherwise.
